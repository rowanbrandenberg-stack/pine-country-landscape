What a Pine Country cleanup includes

  • Pine needles and cones raked out of beds, rock and lawn
  • Leaves and winter debris cleared from beds, walks and gravel
  • Weeds pulled or trimmed, including mullein and thistle
  • Shrubs shaped and dead wood cut out
  • Walks, drives and patios blown clean
  • Everything hauled away in our dump trailer, not left in bags

Fire season matters here

Flagstaff sits in one of the largest ponderosa forests in the world. Built up needles against siding, decks and fences are fuel. A fall or spring cleanup that clears the first 5 feet around your house is one of the easiest things you can do to lower wildfire risk, and it is the first step in Firewise defensible space guidance.

Pruning that does not leave things bare

We shape shrubs and small trees for health and form, not into boxes. Dead, crossing and rubbing branches come out first, then we shape for natural growth. Timing matters at elevation: most pruning here is best in late winter or right after spring bloom.

Recurring care

Want it to stay finished? We set up recurring mowing, trimming and bed care through the growing season, then roll you into winterization and winter watering in the fall. One crew, one number, all year.

Questions

Yard cleanup, answered.

How much does a yard cleanup cost in Flagstaff?

Most cleanups start at $450 for a typical Flagstaff lot, including hauling. Heavy needle buildup, large lots or overgrown properties are quoted after a quick look, and the price is confirmed with you before we start.

Do you haul everything away?

Yes. Debris goes in our dump trailer and leaves with us. Nothing is left in bags at the curb.

When is the best time for a cleanup?

Fall, after the ponderosas drop needles and before the first snow, and spring once the snow melts. Both are busy, so book early.

Can you remove pine needles from rock beds?

Yes. We rake and blow needles out of rock and gravel without pulling the rock out with them.

Do you offer recurring service?

Yes. Recurring mowing visits start at $170, and we can add bed care and weed control on a schedule.
