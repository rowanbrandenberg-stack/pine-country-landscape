Snow questions

Good to know.

Do you sell season snow contracts?

Not this winter. Flagstaff gets too much snow for a flat season price to make sense, so we work on call from a snow list and price each snowfall.

How does the snow list work?

Send your address and driveway details with the form on this page. A standard driveway (2 car, up to 60 ft) plus walks and steps is $99 a snowfall. Long or rural drives get a price by text. When a storm hits, people on the list get cleared first, in the order they joined.

What do you clear?

Driveways, walks, steps and entries, cleared with a snow blower and shovels.

Do I have to commit to anything?

No. Being on the list is free and you only pay for the snowfalls we clear. Text us any time to come off the list.
