Snow questions

Good to know.

What does a season contract cover?

Your driveway is cleared after every snowfall of 2 inches or more from November 1 to April 15, on our priority route, with no limit on the number of storms. The Full Clear Season plan adds the front walk, steps and entry by hand every storm.

How much is it?

Driveway Season is $800 and Full Clear Season is $1,450, both for a standard residential driveway (2 car, up to 60 feet), paid in 4 equal monthly payments with no deposit. Long, steep or rural drives get a written price after a quick look. On call service is $125 per visit when route capacity allows.

How do I sign up?

Reserve your spot with the form on this page. For a standard driveway you can review and sign your contract online right after. For anything else, Rowan follows up with a price first.

How many spots are there?

Route spots are limited so every client on the route gets cleared promptly. Contract clients are always cleared before on call requests.
