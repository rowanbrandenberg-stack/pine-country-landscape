Christmas tree questions

Something not covered? Text Rowan.

Do you bring the tree, or do I buy it?

Tell us on the form whether you already have a tree. If you do, we bring it home on our trailer. If you still need one, Rowan explains how getting the tree works for your install when Rowan replies, before anything is booked.

How much does a tree install cost?

Rowan replies to your request with a price for your install. It depends on the tree, your area and the access. Nothing is booked or charged until you say yes.

Do I need my own tree stand?

Tell us on the form whether you have a stand or need one. Rowan confirms the options for your install when Rowan replies.

What size trees can you install?

Pick the height you have in mind on the form. Not sure? Choose Not sure, help me choose, add your ceiling height, and Rowan helps you pick.

Do you deliver to my area?

Our service area is Flagstaff and about 25 minutes out: Doney Park, Kachina Village, Mountainaire, Fort Valley, Continental, Cheshire, Timberline, Williams, Bellemont and Parks. A little farther out? Ask anyway.

When can you install my tree?

Pick a preferred day or weekend on the form, or tell us you are flexible. It stays a request until Rowan confirms a date with you. Nothing is reserved before then.

What about stairs or tight spots?

Tell us about stairs, narrow halls or gates on the form, and a photo of the doorway helps. That way Rowan knows before confirming your price.

Do you decorate the tree?

The install covers bringing the tree in, setting it straight in the stand and cleaning up the needles. It does not include lights or decorating. If you would like help with those, mention it and Rowan will tell you if it is possible.

Can you take the tree down after the holidays?

Ask about it in your request. Rowan will tell you whether removal is available for your install and what it would cost.

How do I change my request?

Text (928) 863-8198 or reply to Rowan's message. Nothing is booked until Rowan confirms with you, so changes before then are easy.
