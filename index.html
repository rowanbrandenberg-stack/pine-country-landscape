    Questions

    Good to know.

    How does pricing work?

    Every service lists a starting price for a typical Flagstaff lot. Bigger or more involved jobs get a written, itemized quote after we see the property. The price is confirmed with you before any work starts.

    Do you do snow removal?

    Not this winter. Our snow route launches in winter 2027–28. Join the waitlist on this page and you get first pick of route spots before we open it up.

    How do I pay?

    Card or cash. Most service jobs are paid when the work is done. Larger projects are billed on a schedule written into your proposal.

    What areas do you cover?

    Flagstaff and about 25 minutes out: Doney Park, Kachina Village, Mountainaire, Fort Valley, Continental, Cheshire, Williams and Bellemont.

    What days do you work?

    Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday, 8am to 5pm.

    When should I winterize my sprinklers?

    Early to mid October, or as soon as overnight lows start dipping into the 20s. Blowouts start at $165. Irrigation and winterization.

    Do you haul away yard debris?

    Yes. Needles, cones, branches and clippings go in our dump trailer and leave with us. Nothing is left in bags at the curb.

    What trees grow well in Flagstaff?

    Ponderosa and pinyon pine, Colorado blue spruce, and aspen in the right spot do well here. Species choice depends on sun, water and elk pressure, which we work out at a design consultation. Tree planting and design.

    Do I need to water trees in winter?

    Yes, especially new and evergreen trees. Our winters are dry and windy between storms. We offer $95 winter watering visits on warmer days.

    Can I change or cancel my appointment?

    Yes. Reply to your confirmation email or call or text (928) 863-8198 and we will move it.
