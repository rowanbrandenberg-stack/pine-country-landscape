    Questions

    Good to know.

    How do I get a price?

    Book an estimate with the short form on this page, or text (928) 863-8198. Rowan follows up to look at the job with you and gives you the price in writing. There is no charge for an estimate visit, and nothing starts until you say yes.

    Estimate or design consultation, which do I need?

    Most jobs, up to about $3,000, get an estimate: we walk it with you and give you a written price at no charge. Bigger projects like pavers, hardscape and full yard installs start with a 90 minute design consultation on site. It costs $150, and the full $150 is credited toward your project if you go ahead. Not sure which you need? Send a request and Rowan will tell you before anything is booked or charged.

    How does pricing work?

    Our services list starting prices for a typical Flagstaff lot. Bigger or more involved jobs get a written, itemized quote after we see the property. The price is confirmed with you before any work starts.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes. Pine Country Landscape LLC is a licensed Arizona contractor, ROC 368709, classification CR-21 Hardscaping and Irrigation Systems. We carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance and are bonded.

    Do you do snow removal?

    Yes. Season plans for Flagstaff driveways start at $800 for the winter, paid in 4 monthly payments with no deposit. Route spots are limited. See the snow removal page for details and to sign up online.

    What areas do you cover?

    Flagstaff and about 25 minutes out: Doney Park, Kachina Village, Mountainaire, Fort Valley, Continental, Cheshire, Timberline, Williams, Bellemont and Parks.

    What days do you work?

    Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday, 8am to 5pm.

    How do I pay?

    Card or cash. Most service jobs are paid when the work is done. Larger projects are billed on a schedule written into your agreement, which you can review and sign online.
