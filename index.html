    Snow removal · winter 2026

    Wake up to a clear driveway.

    Flagstaff averages close to 100 inches of snow a year. Contract clients are on our priority route, cleared with a compact skid steer and a hand crew for walks and steps, storm after storm, without calling anyone.

    This winter's forecast, briefly

    NOAA expects a very strong El Niño this winter, possibly one of the strongest on record. In northern Arizona, El Niño winters tend to run wetter, with longer stormy stretches and fewer dry breaks between them. Nothing is guaranteed, but the odds lean snowy.

    We are not saying you should sign a snow contract because of the Pacific Ocean. We are saying the Pacific Ocean would.

    Sources: NOAA Climate Prediction Center, Sept 2026 ENSO update. National Weather Service Flagstaff.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    How does pricing work?

    Every service lists a starting price for a typical Flagstaff lot. Bigger or more involved jobs get a written, itemized quote after we see the property. The price is confirmed with you before any work starts.

    How do snow contracts work?

    Season contract clients are on our priority route from November 1 to April 15. When a storm drops more than 2 inches, we clear you automatically. On call pushes are available when the route has room.

    How do I pay?

    Card or cash when the job is done. Larger install projects are billed on a schedule written into your proposal.

    What areas do you cover?

    Flagstaff and about 25 minutes out: Doney Park, Kachina Village, Mountainaire, Fort Valley, Continental, Cheshire, Williams and Bellemont.

    What days do you work?

    Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday, 8am to 5pm. Snow removal runs whenever it snows.

    Can I change or cancel my appointment?

    Yes. Reply to your confirmation email or call or text (928) 863-8198 and we will move it.
