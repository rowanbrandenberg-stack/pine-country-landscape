    Christmas tree questions

    Something not covered? Text Rowan.

    Do you bring the tree, or do I buy it?

    Either works. If you already have a tree, we pick it up and bring it home. If you want us to get one, Rowan finds a tree that fits your size and style, tells you the exact tree and its price, and buys it only after you approve. We never swap in a different tree without asking you.

    How much does a tree install cost?

    The install service is priced by tree height: $99 up to 4 ft, $149 up to 6 ft, $199 up to 8 ft, $299 up to 10 ft, and $399 up to 11 ft after an access review. Taller trees are quoted individually. That covers one pickup, standard local delivery, carrying it in, a fresh cut if needed, setting it in the stand and needle cleanup. The tree and stand are priced individually, and Rowan confirms the exact total before purchase.

    Do I need my own tree stand?

    You can use your own stand once we check that it fits the tree's height and trunk and the stand maker's limits. If it does not fit, or you do not have one, we price a stand for you separately.

    What size trees can you install?

    Pick the height you have in mind on the form. Not sure? Choose Not sure, help me choose, add your ceiling height, and Rowan helps you pick.

    Do you deliver to my area?

    Our service area is Flagstaff and about 25 minutes out: Doney Park, Kachina Village, Mountainaire, Fort Valley, Continental, Cheshire, Timberline, Williams, Bellemont and Parks. A little farther out? Ask anyway.

    When can you install my tree?

    Tree installs run on Saturdays and Sundays. Pick the weekend you want on the form, or tell us any weekend works. It stays a request until Rowan confirms a date with you. Nothing is reserved before then.

    What about stairs or tight spots?

    Tell us about stairs, narrow halls or gates on the form, and a photo of the doorway helps. Rowan reviews it before your price is final, so there are no surprises on install day.

    Can you just deliver the tree?

    Yes. Delivery only is quoted individually, so pick Just deliver it on the form and Rowan sends you a price.

    Do you decorate the tree?

    The install covers bringing the tree in, setting it straight in the stand and cleaning up the needles. It does not include lights or decorating. If you would like help with those, mention it and Rowan will tell you if it is possible.

    Can you take the tree down after the holidays?

    Yes, as an option. Removal is $59 up to 4 ft, $79 up to 6 ft, $99 up to 8 ft, $149 up to 10 ft and $199 up to 11 ft. It is scheduled separately, and choosing it on the form does not reserve a date.

    How do I change my request?

    Text (928) 863-8198 or reply to Rowan's message. Nothing is booked until Rowan confirms with you, so changes before then are easy.
